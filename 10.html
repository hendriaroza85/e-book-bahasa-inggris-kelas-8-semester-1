Narrative Text / Fable

Mengembangkan keterampilan Reading Comprehension (membaca pemahaman) melalui cerita fabel klasik "The Lion and The Mouse". Mempelajari strategi membaca efektif untuk mengidentifikasi struktur, informasi rinci, dan nilai moral dari sebuah teks naratif.

Yuni Rahmi, S.Pd MTsN 10 Agam

A. Ringkasan Cerita: "The Lion and The Mouse"

"The Lion and The Mouse" adalah salah satu fabel paling terkenal yang dikreditkan kepada Aesop. Cerita ini mengisahkan tentang seekor singa yang perkasa yang memutuskan untuk mengampuni nyawa seekor tikus kecil. Sebagai balasan, tikus tersebut kemudian menyelamatkan sang singa dari jerat pemburu dengan menggerogoti talinya.

📌 Tema Utama

  • Mercy (Kasih Sayang) — Kebaikan hati untuk mengampuni mereka yang lebih lemah.
  • Reciprocity (Timbal Balik) — Kebaikan yang kita berikan akan kembali kepada kita.
  • Size Doesn't Matter — Bahkan makhluk terkecil pun bisa memberikan dampak yang besar.

B. Teks Bacaan: "The Lion and The Mouse"

Bacalah teks berikut dengan saksama. Perhatikan penanda struktur (Orientation, Complication, Resolution, Coda) dan kosakata baru di dalamnya.

OrientationOnce upon a time, a mighty lion was sleeping peacefully in the forest. A little mouse, not knowing any better, began running up and down upon the lion's back. This playful action awakened the lion, who placed his huge paw upon the tiny mouse and opened his big jaws to swallow him.

Complication"Pardon, O King," cried the little mouse in fear. "Forgive me this time. I shall never forget it. Who knows but what I may be able to do you a turn some of these days?" The lion was so tickled at the idea of the tiny mouse being able to help the King of Beasts that he lifted his paw and let him go.

Some time later, the lion's pride was his downfall. A few hunters captured the mighty lion and tied him securely to a tree with strong ropes. They left him there while they went to search for a wagon to carry him away.

ResolutionJust then, the little mouse happened to pass by the same path. On seeing the lion's plight, he recognized his friend. Without hesitation, he ran up to the lion and began to gnaw away the thick ropes that bound the great beast. It took some time, but his sharp teeth finally broke the ropes, setting the lion free.

Coda"Was I not right?" said the little mouse with a smile. "Little friends may prove great friends." The moral of the story is: No act of kindness, no matter how small, is ever wasted.

C. Strategi Membaca (Reading Strategies)

📌 Tips for Reading Narrative Text

  • Skimming — Baca cepat paragraf pertama dan terakhir untuk mendapatkan gambaran umum (main idea) dan moral cerita.
  • Scanning — Cari informasi spesifik seperti nama tokoh, tempat, atau tindakan tertentu (misalnya: "What did the mouse do?").
  • Identify the Structure — Temukan di mana masalah dimulai (Complication) dan bagaimana masalah itu diselesaikan (Resolution).
  • Infer the Moral Value — Pikirkan pesan apa yang ingin disampaikan penulis melalui tindakan para tokoh.
  • Guess Meaning from Context — Jika menemukan kata yang tidak diketahui (misalnya: "gnaw"), lihat kalimat sekitarnya untuk menebak artinya (berkaitan dengan "ropes" dan "sharp teeth").

1. What is the text mainly about? 10 POIN
2. Why did the lion let the mouse go? 10 POIN
3. What did the hunters do to the lion? 10 POIN
4. How did the mouse save the lion? 10 POIN
5. What is the moral value of the story? 10 POIN
6. "Once upon a time, a mighty lion was sleeping..." The synonym of the underlined word is... 10 POIN

✍️ Bagian B: Essay

Essay 1: Mention and explain the generic structures of the text "The Lion and The Mouse"! Provide one example sentence from the text for each structure! 20 POIN
Essay 2: What is the moral value of the story? Explain how you can apply this moral value in your daily life at school or at home! Give at least 2 concrete examples! 20 POIN
Essay 3: Mention 5 (five) vocabulary words from the reading text and write their meanings in Indonesian! 20 POIN
Essay 4: Why did the lion laugh (was tickled) when the mouse said he could help him someday? What does this tell us about the lion's initial perception of the mouse? 20 POIN
Essay 5: Compare "The Lion and The Mouse" with "The Ant and The Dove". What is the similarity in their moral values? Explain your answer! 20 POIN
