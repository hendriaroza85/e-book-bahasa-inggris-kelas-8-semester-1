Narrative Text / Fable

Mengembangkan keterampilan Speaking melalui cerita fabel klasik "Androcles and The Lion". Mempraktikkan dialog, pelafalan (pronunciation), intonasi, dan teknik bercerita lisan (storytelling) dengan percaya diri.

Yuni Rahmi, S.Pd MTsN 10 Agam

A. Ringkasan Cerita: "Androcles and The Lion"

"Androcles and The Lion" adalah sebuah fabel klasik yang berasal dari cerita rakyat Romawi kuno. Cerita ini menceritakan tentang seorang budak bernama Androcles yang melarikan diri dari tuannya ke hutan. Di sana, ia menemukan seekor singa yang kesakitan karena ada duri besar di kakinya. Androcles dengan berani membantu mencabut duri tersebut, dan mereka menjadi sahabat. Beberapa waktu kemudian, Androcles ditangkap dan harus berhadapan dengan singa di arena. Ternyata, singa itu adalah singa yang sama yang pernah ia tolong.

📌 Tema Utama

  • Gratitude (Rasa Syukur) — Hewan pun bisa mengenali dan membalas kebaikan.
  • Compassion (Welas Asih) — Menolong sesama meskipun berbeda spesies.
  • Friendship (Persahabatan) — Persahabatan sejati melampaui batas.

B. Simulasi Dialog Speaking

Praktikkan dialog berikut bersama teman sebangku Anda. Satu orang berperan sebagai Androcles, satu sebagai Narrator, dan satu sebagai The Lion. Perhatikan intonasi dan ekspresi emosi!

[Scene 1: In the Forest — Androcles meets the injured Lion]

Narrator: "One day, a poor slave named Androcles ran away from his cruel master. He hid in a deep, dark forest. Suddenly, he heard a loud groan behind a bush."

Androcles: (whispering) "What was that sound? Oh my God! It's a lion! But... wait, it seems to be in pain."

The Lion: (groaning) "Ooooh... help me... please... I'm in so much pain..."

Androcles: (bravely approaching) "Don't be afraid, dear lion. I won't hurt you. Let me see... Oh! There's a big thorn in your paw. Let me help you."

[Androcles carefully pulls out the thorn]

The Lion: (relieved) "Thank you, kind human! You have saved my life. I will never forget your goodness."

Androcles: "You're welcome, dear friend. We will always be friends."

[Scene 2: Many years later — In the Arena]

Narrator: "Many years passed. Androcles was caught and thrown into the arena to face a hungry lion. The crowd watched eagerly. But something amazing happened..."

Androcles: (trembling) "This is the end... The lion is going to eat me..."

The Lion: (recognizing Androcles, licking his face gently) "My dear friend! I remember you! You are the one who saved me!"

Androcles: (surprised and happy) "You... you remember me? You are the same lion I helped in the forest!"

Narrator: "The lion did not attack Androcles. Instead, he hugged him like a long-lost friend. The crowd was amazed. The emperor decided to free them both."

Narrator: "And so, gratitude is the sign of noble souls. Even a wild lion can remember kindness."

C. Tips Speaking untuk Fable

📌 Tips for Speaking a Fable Story

  • Use different voices for characters — Gunakan suara yang berbeda untuk setiap tokoh (misalnya suara lembut untuk Androcles, suara berat untuk Singa, suara narator yang jelas).
  • Express emotions — Tunjukkan emosi yang sesuai: takut, sedih, lega, bahagia, terkejut.
  • Pay attention to pronunciation — Perhatikan pelafalan kata-kata sulit seperti Androcles (/ˈændrəkliːz/), thorn (/θɔːrn/), groan (/ɡroʊn/).
  • Use gestures and body language — Gunakan gerakan tangan dan ekspresi wajah untuk membuat cerita lebih hidup.
  • Maintain good pacing — Jangan terlalu cepat. Berikan jeda pada momen dramatis.
  • Use appropriate intonation — Naikkan intonasi saat bertanya atau kaget, turunkan saat menceritakan fakta.

D. Useful Expressions

💬 Expressions for Offering and Accepting Help

  • Offering help: "Let me help you." / "Can I help you?" / "Do you need any help?"
  • Accepting help: "Yes, please." / "Thank you so much." / "I really appreciate it."
  • Expressing gratitude: "Thank you for saving my life." / "I will never forget your kindness."
  • Expressing surprise: "Oh my God!" / "I can't believe it!" / "What a surprise!"

🎭 Expressions for Storytelling

  • Opening: "Once upon a time..." / "Long ago, there lived..."
  • Sequencing: "One day..." / "Suddenly..." / "Then..." / "After that..."
  • Describing emotions: "He was terrified." / "She felt relieved." / "They were overjoyed."
  • Closing: "And they lived happily ever after." / "The moral of the story is..."
