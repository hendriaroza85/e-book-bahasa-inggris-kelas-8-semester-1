Narrative Text / Folktale

Mengembangkan keterampilan Speaking melalui cerita rakyat (folktale) klasik "The Wise Man". Mempraktikkan dialog, pelafalan (pronunciation), intonasi, dan teknik bercerita lisan (storytelling) dengan ekspresi yang tepat.

Yuni Rahmi, S.Pd MTsN 10 Agam

A. Pengertian Folktale

Folktale (cerita rakyat) adalah jenis Narrative Text yang diwariskan secara lisan dari generasi ke generasi dalam suatu masyarakat. Cerita ini sering kali anonim (tidak diketahui penulis aslinya) dan mengandung nilai-nilai budaya, kearifan lokal, atau pelajaran moral yang universal.

📌 Ciri-Ciri Folktale

  • Dimulai dengan frasa pembuka klasik seperti "Once upon a time..." atau "Long ago..."
  • Tokohnya bisa berupa manusia biasa, raja, atau tokoh bijaksana (wise man).
  • Berisi pesan moral atau kearifan hidup yang mendalam.
  • Alur cerita sederhana namun penuh makna.

B. Simulasi Dialog Speaking: "The Wise Man"

Praktikkan dialog berikut bersama teman sebangku Anda. Satu orang berperan sebagai Narrator, satu sebagai Wise Man, dan satu sebagai Young Man. Perhatikan intonasi dan ekspresi emosi!

[Scene 1: In the village — The Young Man complains to the Wise Man]

Narrator: "Once upon a time, in a peaceful village, lived an old wise man. Everyone respected him for his calmness and wisdom. One day, a young man came to him, looking very angry and frustrated."

Young Man: (complaining loudly) "Life is so hard! I have so much pain and trouble. I can't take it anymore!"

Wise Man: (calmly) "I understand your pain, my son. Let me teach you a lesson. Please go to the kitchen and bring me a handful of salt and a glass of water."

[The young man brings the salt and water]

Wise Man: "Now, put the salt into the glass of water and drink it. How does it taste?"

Young Man: (spitting it out) "It tastes bitter and very salty! It's awful!"

[Scene 2: By the lake]

Narrator: "The wise man smiled gently and asked the young man to follow him to a nearby large lake."

Wise Man: "Now, throw another handful of salt into this lake, and then drink the water from the lake. How does it taste?"

Young Man: (drinking and smiling) "It tastes fresh and cool. It's delicious!"

Wise Man: "Did you taste the salt this time?"

Young Man: "No, I didn't."

Wise Man: (wisely) "The pain in life is like pure salt. The amount of pain remains the same, but the bitterness depends on the container. So, stop being a glass. Become a lake."

Narrator: "The young man finally understood. He bowed respectfully and went home with a peaceful heart."

C. Tips Speaking untuk Folktale

📌 Tips for Speaking a Folktale Story

  • Master the pacing — Gunakan tempo yang lambat dan tenang saat menjadi "Wise Man", dan tempo yang lebih cepat/emosional saat menjadi "Young Man".
  • Use appropriate facial expressions — Tunjukkan ekspresi frustrasi, tenang, terkejut, dan akhirnya lega/paham.
  • Emphasize key words — Tekankan kata-kata penting seperti "bitter", "fresh", dan "become a lake".
  • Use hand gestures — Gunakan gerakan tangan untuk memperagakan tindakan (misalnya: menabur garam, minum air).
  • Maintain eye contact — Tatap lawan bicara atau audiens untuk menyampaikan pesan moral dengan lebih menyentuh.

D. Useful Expressions

💬 Expressions for Complaining and Advising

  • Complaining: "Life is so hard!" / "I can't take it anymore!" / "I have so many problems."
  • Asking for advice: "What should I do?" / "Can you help me with this?"
  • Giving advice (Wisely): "Let me teach you a lesson." / "You should broaden your mind." / "Try to look at it from a different perspective."
  • Expressing realization: "Now I understand." / "That makes sense." / "Thank you for the wisdom."
