Narrative Text / Fairytale

Memahami teks Narrative jenis Fairytale (dongeng) melalui keterampilan menyimak (Listening). Mempelajari struktur, ciri kebahasaan, dan nilai moral dari cerita dongeng klasik "A Brave Girl".

Yuni Rahmi, S.Pd MTsN 10 Agam

A. Pengertian Fairytale

Fairytale adalah jenis Narrative Text yang berisi cerita imajinatif atau dongeng yang sering kali melibatkan unsur magis, peri, penyihir, raksasa, atau hewan yang bisa berbicara. Berbeda dengan Fable yang tokohnya hewan, Fairytale biasanya berpusat pada manusia (seringkali seorang pahlawan atau putri) yang menghadapi tantangan besar.

📌 Ciri-Ciri Fairytale

  • Dimulai dengan frasa klasik seperti "Once upon a time..." atau "Long ago..."
  • Memiliki unsur magis atau fantasi (magic, spells, enchanted objects).
  • Tokoh utama biasanya menghadapi ujian atau tantangan berat.
  • Selalu diakhiri dengan happy ending dan pesan moral yang kuat.

B. Struktur Generic Narrative Text

1. Orientation (Pengenalan)
Memperkenalkan tokoh, latar waktu, dan tempat.
Contoh: "Once upon a time, in a small village, lived a brave girl named Maya."
2. Complication (Konflik)
Munculnya masalah atau tantangan yang harus dihadapi tokoh utama.
Contoh: "One day, a fierce tiger threatened the villagers' livestock."
3. Resolution (Penyelesaian)
Tokoh utama menemukan cara untuk mengatasi masalah tersebut.
Contoh: "Maya bravely set a trap and caught the tiger safely."
4. Coda / Moral Value (Pesan Moral)
Pelajaran yang dapat diambil dari cerita.
Contoh: "True bravery is facing fear to protect others."

C. Simulasi Teks Listening: "A Brave Girl"

Bacalah teks berikut dengan saksama. Bayangkan Anda sedang mendengarkan rekaman suara guru Anda membacakan dongeng ini. Perhatikan struktur dan ciri kebahasaannya.

[Orientation] Once upon a time, in a small village surrounded by a dense, magical forest, lived a young girl named Maya. She was not the strongest or the tallest, but she was known for her sharp mind and brave heart. The villagers loved her because she was always ready to help others.

[Complication] One day, a fierce and hungry tiger started wandering near the village. It attacked the villagers' livestock at night. The villagers were terrified. They held a meeting and decided that the only solution was to abandon their homes and move to another place. However, Maya refused to give up. She believed there was a better way to protect her home.

[Resolution] That night, Maya gathered some strong ropes and a large net. She hid quietly near the livestock pen, waiting patiently. When the tiger approached, she did not run away. Instead, she bravely threw the heavy net over the tiger and tied the ropes securely. The tiger roared and struggled, but it could not escape. The next morning, the villagers were amazed. They called the forest rangers, who safely transported the tiger to a wildlife sanctuary far away.

[Coda] Maya was celebrated as a hero in her village. She proved that you do not need to be big or strong to make a difference. The moral of the story is: True bravery is not the absence of fear, but the willingness to face it to protect what you love.

D. Kosakata Penting (Key Vocabulary)

  • Dense = Lebat / Padat
  • Fierce = Ganas / Buas
  • Livestock = Hewan ternak
  • Terrified = Sangat ketakutan
  • Abandon = Meninggalkan
  • Patiently = Dengan sabar
  • Securely = Dengan aman / erat
  • Sanctuary = Tempat perlindungan / suaka
  • Absence = Ketiadaan
  • Willingness = Kesediaan / kemauan
