A. Ringkasan Cerita: "The Brave Knight"

"The Brave Knight" adalah sebuah cerita rakyat (folktale) yang mengisahkan tentang seorang ksatria muda bernama Sir Arthur. Berbeda dengan ksatria lain yang mengandalkan kekuatan fisik, Sir Arthur dikenal karena keberanian yang dipadukan dengan kebijaksanaan dan welas asih. Ketika sebuah desa terancam oleh makhluk yang menakutkan, ia tidak langsung menyerang, melainkan mencari akar permasalahan dan menemukan solusi damai yang menyelamatkan semua pihak.

📌 Tema Utama

  • True Bravery (Keberanian Sejati) — Keberanian bukan hanya tentang bertarung, tetapi juga tentang melindungi yang lemah.
  • Wisdom over Force (Kebijaksanaan di atas Kekuatan) — Masalah besar sering kali bisa diselesaikan dengan pemikiran yang tenang, bukan kekerasan.
  • Compassion (Welas Asih) — Memahami sudut pandang pihak lain, bahkan musuh sekalipun.

B. Teks Bacaan: "The Brave Knight"

Bacalah teks berikut dengan saksama. Perhatikan penanda struktur (Orientation, Complication, Resolution, Coda) dan kosakata baru di dalamnya.

OrientationOnce upon a time, in the peaceful kingdom of Avalon, lived a young and brave knight named Sir Arthur. He was not the tallest or the strongest knight in the kingdom, but he was known for his kind heart and sharp mind. The villagers loved him because he always protected the weak and helped those in need.

ComplicationOne day, a fearsome creature began attacking the villages near the castle. It stole the farmers' livestock and destroyed their crops at night. The king was furious and promised a great reward to any knight who could defeat the creature. Many brave knights went to the forest with their swords, but they all returned in fear, saying the creature was too powerful to fight.

Instead of rushing into battle, Sir Arthur decided to observe the creature first. He hid in the forest for three nights. On the fourth night, he discovered the truth: the creature was not evil. It was a mother dragon who had lost her babies, and she was stealing food because she was starving and desperate.

ResolutionSir Arthur did not draw his sword. Instead, he went back to the castle and explained the situation to the king. He proposed a peaceful solution. The king agreed to provide a large amount of food for the dragon and ordered the villagers to build a safe, warm cave for her in the deep mountains, far from the farms. When the dragon found the new cave and the food, she stopped attacking the villages. She even became a guardian of the kingdom's borders.

CodaSir Arthur was celebrated as the bravest knight in the land. He proved that true bravery is not about how hard you can hit, but about having the courage to find a peaceful and wise solution. The moral of the story is: Wisdom and compassion are the greatest weapons of a true hero.

C. Strategi Membaca (Reading Strategies)

📌 Tips for Reading Narrative Text

  • Identify the Moral Value — Cari kalimat di akhir cerita (Coda) yang biasanya berisi pesan moral atau pelajaran hidup.
  • Analyze Character Traits — Perhatikan tindakan dan dialog tokoh untuk memahami sifat mereka (misalnya: Sir Arthur yang observant dan compassionate).
  • Map the Plot — Tentukan di mana masalah dimulai (Complication) dan bagaimana masalah tersebut diselesaikan tanpa kekerasan (Resolution).
  • Context Clues — Gunakan kalimat di sekitarnya untuk menebak arti kata sulit. Contoh: "starving and desperate" memberikan petunjuk tentang kondisi sang naga.
