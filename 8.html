Narrative Text / Fable

Memahami teks Narrative jenis Fable melalui keterampilan menyimak (Listening). Mempelajari struktur, ciri kebahasaan, dan nilai moral dari cerita fabel klasik "The Ant and The Dove" karya Aesop.

Yuni Rahmi, S.Pd MTsN 10 Agam

A. Pengertian Narrative Text dan Fable

Narrative Text adalah teks yang bertujuan untuk menghibur pembaca/pendengar (to amuse or entertain) dengan menceritakan sebuah kisah yang memiliki komplikasi (masalah) dan resolusi (penyelesaian).

📌 Apa itu Fable?

  • Fable adalah jenis Narrative Text yang tokoh-tokohnya berupa hewan yang berperilaku seperti manusia (personification).
  • Fable selalu mengandung nilai moral (moral value/lesson) di akhir cerita.
  • Contoh fabel terkenal: The Ant and The Dove, The Tortoise and The Hare, The Lion and The Mouse.
  • Fabel berasal dari tradisi lisan dan sering dikaitkan dengan Aesop, seorang pendongeng Yunani kuno.

B. Struktur Generic Narrative Text

Berbeda dengan Recount Text, Narrative Text memiliki struktur yang lebih kompleks karena adanya konflik dan penyelesaian:

1. Orientation (Pengenalan)
Memperkenalkan tokoh (characters), latar waktu (when), dan latar tempat (where).
Contoh: "One hot day, an ant was searching for some water..."
2. Complication (Konflik/Masalah)
Bagian di mana masalah atau konflik muncul. Ini adalah inti dari cerita narrative.
Contoh: "The ant fell into the water and was about to drown..."
3. Resolution (Penyelesaian)
Masalah terselesaikan, bisa berakhir bahagia (happy ending) atau sedih (sad ending).
Contoh: "The dove dropped a leaf into the water, and the ant climbed onto it..."
4. Coda / Reorientation (Pesan Moral — Opsional)
Pesan moral atau pelajaran yang bisa dipetik dari cerita. Tidak semua narrative memiliki bagian ini secara eksplisit.
Contoh: "One good turn deserves another."

C. Ciri Kebahasaan (Language Features)

📌 Language Features of Narrative Text

  • Menggunakan Simple Past Tense (V2) karena menceritakan kisah di masa lalu.
  • Menggunakan Past Continuous Tense untuk menggambarkan latar suasana (e.g., "The sun was shining brightly...").
  • Menggunakan Temporal Conjunctions: one day, once upon a time, then, suddenly, finally.
  • Menggunakan Action Verbs: ran, jumped, flew, fell, caught, saved.
  • Menggunakan Saying Verbs (dialog langsung): said, cried, shouted, whispered.
  • Menggunakan Thinking/Feeling Verbs: felt, thought, realized, decided.
  • Menggunakan Third Person Pronoun: he, she, it, they (karena tokoh biasanya hewan).

D. Simulasi Teks Listening: "The Ant and The Dove"

Bacalah teks berikut dengan saksama. Bayangkan Anda sedang mendengarkan rekaman suara guru Anda membacakan cerita fabel ini. Perhatikan struktur dan ciri kebahasaannya.

[Orientation] One hot summer day, a little ant was searching for some water. After walking around for a long time, she came to a spring. To reach the spring, she had to climb up a blade of grass. While making her way up, she slipped and fell into the water.

[Complication] The ant was drowning in the water. She struggled hard, but the current was too strong for her tiny body. She cried for help, but no one seemed to hear her. A dove, sitting on a nearby tree, saw the ant in trouble. The dove felt sorry for the little creature and decided to help her.

[Resolution] The dove quickly plucked a large leaf from the tree and dropped it into the water near the struggling ant. The ant climbed onto the leaf, and the leaf floated safely to the dry ground. The ant was saved! She looked up at the dove and thanked her from the bottom of her heart.

Not long after that, a bird hunter arrived in the forest. He saw the beautiful dove sitting on the tree and aimed his net at her. The ant, who was still nearby, saw the danger. She quickly bit the hunter's foot as hard as she could. The hunter cried out in pain and dropped his net. The dove heard the commotion, spread her wings, and flew away to safety.

[Coda] The dove and the ant became best friends forever. The moral of the story is: "One good turn deserves another." If you help others, they will help you in return.

E. Kosakata Penting (Key Vocabulary)

  • Spring = Mata air
  • Blade of grass = Helai rumput
  • Slipped = Terpeleset
  • Drowning = Tenggelam
  • Struggled = Berjuang / Berusaha keras
  • Current = Arus
  • Plucked = Memetik
  • Floated = Mengapung
  • Hunter = Pemburu
  • Aimed = Membidik
  • Commotion = Kegaduhan
  • Deserves = Pantas / Layak
