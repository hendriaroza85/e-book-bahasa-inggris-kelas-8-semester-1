Recount Text

Mengembangkan keterampilan Speaking (bercerita lisan) dan Reading (membaca pemahaman) teks Recount. Mempelajari strategi membaca efektif dan teknik menceritakan kembali pengalaman secara lisan dengan percaya diri.

Yuni Rahmi, S.Pd MTsN 10 Agam

A. Reading Comprehension

Bacalah teks recount berikut dengan saksama. Perhatikan struktur, ciri kebahasaan, dan informasi penting di dalamnya.

My Unforgettable School Camp Experience

Last month, my school organized a camping activity for all eighth-grade students. It was held at Mount Merapi National Park from Friday to Sunday. I was very excited because it was my first time joining a school camp.

On Friday morning, we gathered at the school yard at 6 AM. After roll call and briefing from the teachers, we departed by bus. The journey took about three hours. When we arrived, we immediately set up our tents. I was in group 3 with five other friends. We worked together to pitch our tent, and it was quite challenging but fun.

In the afternoon, we participated in various outdoor activities. First, we went on a nature trail and learned about different types of plants and trees. Then, we practiced orienteering using a compass and map. It was my first time using a compass, and I found it very interesting. After that, we had a team-building game called "Human Knot." We had to untangle ourselves without letting go of each other's hands. It was hilarious and brought us closer together.

That night, we had a campfire gathering. Each group performed songs and dances. My group sang "Laskar Pelangi" together. After that, the teachers told scary stories around the campfire. Some of my friends were too scared to sleep alone in their tents!

On Saturday, we woke up early to watch the sunrise. It was breathtaking to see the golden light spreading over the mountains. After breakfast, we went hiking to the summit. It was a steep climb, but we encouraged each other along the way. When we reached the top, the view was absolutely stunning. We took many photos to capture the moment.

On Sunday morning, we packed our belongings and cleaned the camping area. Before leaving, we had a farewell ceremony. The headmaster gave a speech about the importance of teamwork and perseverance. Then, we returned to school.

It was an unforgettable experience. I learned many valuable lessons about cooperation, courage, and appreciating nature. I also made new friends and strengthened my bond with my classmates. I hope I can join another camping trip next year.

B. Speaking: Telling a Recount Story

Menceritakan kembali pengalaman secara lisan memerlukan keterampilan khusus. Berikut adalah tips untuk menjadi storyteller yang baik:

📌 Tips for Speaking a Recount Text

  • Use clear pronunciation — Ucapkan setiap kata dengan jelas, terutama akhiran -ed pada regular verbs.
  • Maintain good intonation — Gunakan intonasi yang bervariasi agar cerita tidak terdengar monoton.
  • Use temporal conjunctions — Gunakan kata hubung waktu seperti first, then, after that, finally untuk menunjukkan urutan kejadian.
  • Make eye contact — Tatap audiens Anda untuk membangun koneksi.
  • Use gestures and expressions — Gunakan gerakan tangan dan ekspresi wajah untuk membuat cerita lebih hidup.
  • Speak at a moderate pace — Jangan terlalu cepat atau terlalu lambat.
  • Practice before performing — Latih cerita Anda beberapa kali sebelum menceritakannya di depan kelas.

💡 Useful Expressions for Recount Speaking

  • Opening: "I'd like to tell you about..." / "Let me share my experience of..."
  • Sequencing: "First,... Then,... After that,... Finally,..."
  • Adding details: "What happened next was..." / "The most interesting part was..."
  • Closing: "That was my experience." / "It was truly unforgettable because..."

C. Reading Strategies for Recount Text

Untuk memahami Recount Text dengan baik, gunakan strategi membaca berikut:

📌 Effective Reading Strategies

  • Skimming — Baca cepat untuk mendapatkan gambaran umum (main idea) teks.
  • Scanning — Cari informasi spesifik (nama, tempat, waktu, angka) dengan cepat.
  • Identify the structure — Temukan bagian Orientation, Events, dan Reorientation.
  • Look for temporal conjunctions — Perhatikan kata-kata seperti first, then, after that, finally untuk memahami urutan kejadian.
  • Understand the context — Gunakan konteks kalimat untuk memahami kata-kata yang tidak diketahui.
  • Identify the writer's feelings — Perhatikan kata-kata yang menunjukkan emosi atau kesan penulis.
