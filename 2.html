The Champion of Panjat Pinang

Fokus pada keterampilan berbicara (Speaking). Mempraktikkan dialog, ungkapan memberi dan meminta pendapat, serta pelafalan (pronunciation) dan intonasi yang tepat saat menceritakan kembali pengalaman masa lalu.

Yuni Rahmi, S.Pd MTsN 10 Agam

A. Simulasi Dialog Speaking

Praktikkan dialog berikut bersama teman sebangku Anda. Perhatikan intonasi dan pelafalan kata yang dicetak tebal.

Raka: "Hey, Dina! What do you think about the Panjat Pinang competition in our village last week?"

Dina: "I think it was very exciting! The team 'Garuda Muda' was amazing. They climbed the pole so fast."

Raka: "I agree with you. But they slipped twice before reaching the top. They were very brave."

Dina: "Yes, they showed great teamwork. We should learn from their perseverance."

B. Konsep Penting Speaking

📌 Asking and Giving Opinion

  • Asking: "What do you think about...?" / "What is your opinion on...?"
  • Giving: "I think..." / "In my opinion..." / "I believe that..."

📌 Pronunciation Tips (-ed Endings)

Pelafalan akhiran -ed pada Regular Verbs:
• Suara /t/ setelah huruf tak bersuara (e.g., slipped, watched).
• Suara /d/ setelah huruf bersuara (e.g., climbed, played).
• Suara /ɪd/ setelah huruf 't' atau 'd' (e.g., wanted, needed).
